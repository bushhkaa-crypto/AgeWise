Privacy Policy

Welcome to AgeWise. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information. AgeWise does not collect, store, or share any personal data. By using AgeWise, you agree to this policy.

1

Introduction

AgeWise is a simple age calculator app designed to respect and protect your personal information. This policy describes what information we do (and do not) handle, and how your data is treated while using the app.

2

Information We Do NOT Collect

AgeWise does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your date of birth (it stays only on your device)
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your contacts or photos
  • Any device identifiers or usage data

Your data never leaves your device.

3

Date of Birth Usage

The date of birth you enter is used only on your device to calculate your age in years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds. It is never sent to any server, cloud, or third party. It is not saved after you close the app.

4

Permissions

AgeWise does not request any device permissions. The app does not access:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Location services
  • Storage or files
  • Any other sensitive data
5

Data Storage

No data is stored anywhere outside your device. Once you close the app, the entered information is cleared and nothing is saved permanently. There are no databases, no cloud storage, and no backups.

6

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs that collect your information. AgeWise is completely free of ads and trackers.

7

Internet Connection

AgeWise works completely offline. It does not require an internet connection and does not send any data over the network. You can use the app in airplane mode without any issues.

8

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, AgeWise is safe for users of all ages.

9

Data Security

Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of data breach or misuse. Your information stays only on your device and is never exposed to anyone.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
